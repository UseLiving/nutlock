# NUTLOCK ($NUTLOCK)

The No Nut November holdout map. Pick a squad, take the pledge, and keep the acorn locked for 30 days.

**Live site:** https://useliving.github.io/nutlock/  
**X:** https://x.com/UseNutlock

## What you can do
- Explore the holdout map: squads as colonies, every dot a pledger (gold holding, green new, grey fell)
- Zoom, pan, drag squads around, search, and open any squad for its survival stats
- Scrub the time machine through November to watch the map hold up
- Take the pledge to get your own dot, check in daily, and copy a brag to post
- Play Resist: pop temptations before they reach Nutty's acorn
- Pay respects on the Wall of F

Single self-contained `index.html`. A meme site: squads and pledgers are simulated, your pledge is saved in your browser.
